03 / CLEAR SPACE & MINIMUM SIZE

Clear Space Rule

To ensure the logo retains maximum visual impact and legibility, always maintain a clear zone around it. No text, graphic elements, or image focal points should intrude into this boundary.

Minimum Reproducible Size

To preserve sharp clarity across print and digital media, do not render the primary logo below these established minimum dimensions:

  • Digital / Web: 80px wide
  • Print Collateral: 25mm wide
  • Large Format / Signage: 150mm wide
